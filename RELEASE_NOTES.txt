1. HIGHLIGHTS & WHAT'S NEW
------------------------------------------------------------
• Shift Route & Live Fleet Radar Auto-Defaulting Fix:
  - Eliminated automatic defaulting to in-office stationary personnel (Pandeeshwari S).
  - Implemented Intelligent Multi-Tier Fleet Priority Ranking:
    * Tier 1: Actively moving field staff (speed > 0.8 km/h, moving = true) -> Rank #1 (e.g. Praveen B, Saravanakumar B).
    * Tier 2: Field staff in transit with recorded displacement > 100m.
    * Tier 3: Other field staff with live satellite ping.
    * Tier 4: In-office staff (checked in via biometric / HQ geofence).
    * Tier 5: Idle / standby staff with morning check-in.
    * Tier 6: Offline / awaiting shift punch.
    * Tier 7: Clocked-out staff (GPS coordinates zeroed for privacy).
    * Tier 8: Staff on approved leave (dedicated privacy card).
  - Live Radar map canvas automatically auto-centers and focuses on the leading moving field executive with full route displacement polyline and travel allowance pay.

• Authoritative Shift-Bound Real-Time GPS Tracking:
  - Tracking strictly operates between Shift Start (Check-In) and Shift End (Check-Out).
  - When employee checks in (biometric machine in office, FaceAttend terminal, or mobile app), tracking begins immediately.
  - If employee has NOT checked in for the day, telemetry coordinates are suppressed and employee is displayed as "Awaiting Punch" / "Offline".
  - Upon Check-Out (biometric punch-out or mobile clock-out), tracking terminates immediately, zeroing coordinates to guarantee statutory employee privacy.
  - Mobile background service (`shift_tracking_service.dart`) strictly gates location stream on `isCheckedIn && isShiftActive`.

• Sidebar Navigation & Restful REST Slug Desync Resolution:
  - Resolved route collisions between `/biometric/gps` and `/gps-travel-fare`.
  - Clicking "Field Travel & KM Fare Config" in the sidebar directly opens `FieldTravelKmFareTab` without inheriting stale `?subTab=tracking`.
  - Added canonical routes and aliases in `urlRouter.ts` and `routeRegistry.ts` for clean back/forward browser navigation.

• Re-organized Clocking & Devices Navigation Order:
  - JOY FaceAttend (Add-On)
  - Biometric Devices
  - Biometric Rules & Automation
  - GPS & Mobile Clocking
  - Geofence Boundaries
  - Shift Route & Live Fleet Radar (Live)
  - Field Travel & KM Fare Config

• Field Travel & KM Fare Reimbursement Engine:
  - Configurable vehicle mileage rates:
    * 2-Wheeler : ₹4.50 / km
    * 4-Wheeler : ₹10.00 / km
    * Standard Base : ₹6.00 / km
  - Real-time travel reimbursement pay computation with 1-click payroll sync.

• On-Leave Privacy Shield:
  - Approved leave records (e.g., Shalini J) render a dedicated Privacy Protected Leave Card rather than exposing geographic coordinates or an empty map canvas.
