1. Executive Summary

Joy PeopleHR (WorkForceOS Enterprise) is a comprehensive cloud Human Resource Management and Contractor Governance SaaS platform. It combines direct employee payroll, organizational hierarchy, statutory compliance, and third-party contractor manpower governance into a synchronized multi-platform architecture.

2. Architectural Comparison: HR Core vs. Vendor Governance

While both modules share similar data touchpoints (such as Attendance, Wages, and PF/ESI), their business personas and financial executions are distinct:

Functional Dimension 🏢 HR Core (Internal Employees) 🤝 Vendor Governance (Contract Staff)
Employment Status Permanent, direct company payroll. Employed by third-party staffing vendors.
Payroll Execution Company calculates net pay and deposits directly to staff. Vendor pays staff; Company pays Vendor's monthly invoice.
Attendance Purpose Calculates monthly staff pay, paid leaves, and deductions. Gate Pass & Audit: Verifies actual contractor presence to prevent billing inflation.
PF & ESI Compliance Direct compliance filed directly to EPFO/ESIC by the company. Audit Radar: Verifies vendor filed challans before approving final invoice payout (CLRA compliance).
Settlement Flow Direct Payslips (Form 16). 3-Way Match: Purchase Order + Gate Hours + Vendor Invoice.

3. The 4-Stage Vendor Governance Lifecycle

STAGE 1: Onboarding & Licenses --> Labor Licenses (OCR Scanner), Form V & Site Rosters STAGE 2: Operations & Time --> Gate Attendance, Shift Logs & Overtime Audits STAGE 3: Settlement & 3-Way --> Purchase Orders + Actual Wages + Vendor Invoice Match STAGE 4: Compliance & Audit --> PF/ESI Challan Audits, Statutory Returns & Payout Approval
  • 1. 🏢 Vendor & Workforce Hub: Vendor profiles, rate cards, OCR labor license tracker, and contractor site deployments.
  • 2. ⏱️ Contractor Time & Attendance: Biometric/RFID gate check-ins and overtime discrepancy audits.
  • 3. 💳 Settlement & 3-Way Match Engine: Automated validation matching (PO + Verified Attendance + Vendor Invoice).
  • 4. 🛡️ Statutory & Compliance Radar: Form V returns, PF/ESI challan verification, and audit trail logs.

4. Security Architecture & Anti-Session-Hijack Defense

To protect the SaaS production environment against session exfiltration, token theft, and cross-device copying:

  • Cryptographic Device Fingerprint Binding: Generates a hybrid DJB2 + FNV-1a hardware signature from User-Agent, platform, screen resolution, timezone, and language. If tokens are moved to an unauthorized computer, the session is instantly purged.
  • Ephemeral SaaS Storage: Avoids unmanaged persistent local storage in favor of tab-isolated sessionStorage and in-memory Supabase sessions.
  • Enterprise Security Headers: Enforces X-Frame-Options: SAMEORIGIN, X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff, Strict-Transport-Security (HSTS), and strict Referrer Policy on server.js.
  • Dynamic CORS Origin Validation: Strict whitelist restricting access exclusively to https://joypeoplehr.com and mobile application clients.

5. Multi-Platform Real-Time Sync (Web + Flutter)

Both the Web Enterprise App and the Flutter Mobile App connect to the same remote Supabase Enterprise Cloud Database (https://wmqjmyzzamgxyeuotbki.supabase.co). Attendance clock-ins, leave approvals, and employee profile updates replicate in real-time across both platforms.
