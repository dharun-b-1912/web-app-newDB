1. Executive Summary

In Indian workforce operations, overtime calculations frequently create friction between contractual company compensation and statutory labour compliance (Factories Act 1948 Sec. 59, TN Contract Labour Rules Rule 78).

The JOY PeopleHR Dedicated OT Policy Configuration & Calculation Engine eliminates common industry errors (falsified attendance, double-counted ESIC overtime, and unaligned net payouts) by strictly decoupling calculations into two synchronized contexts anchored to immutable biometric attendance:

• ACTUAL ATTENDANCE: 30.00 hrs (Biometric source • Never altered or overwritten)
• COMPANY PAYROLL CONTEXT: Payout = 30 hrs × ₹122.60/hr = ₹3,677.88 (Drives Payslip & Net Take-Home)
• STATUTORY CONTEXT: Rate = ₹163.46/hr (2.0x) • Equivalent = 22.50 hrs • Value = ₹3,677.88 (Reconciliation Variance: ₹0.00)

4. Effective-Dating & Immutability Protection

Historical closed runs are strictly locked:

  • Snapshot Retention: When an August payroll run is locked, the policy configuration version is permanently retained in payroll_ot_calculations.
  • Safe Revisions: When an administrator alters the policy for September (e.g. from 1.50x to Fixed ₹75/hr), historical August and July payrolls are not retroactively recalculated.
  • Audit Trail: Every change is recorded in payroll_ot_policy_audit_logs with administrator identity, timestamp, old values, new values, and reason.
